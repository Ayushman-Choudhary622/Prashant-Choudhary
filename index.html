UX Expert

As a Senior Team Lead UX Designer with over 10 years of experience in Product and UX-UI design, I bring a wealth of expertise to creating human-centric designs and delightful user experiences. My journey began at IIT Hyderabad, where I honed my skills in merging purpose, technology, and inspiration to craft innovative solutions.

My passion lies in guiding cross-functional teams to transform concepts into impactful digital products. From initial sketches to polished 2D/3D outputs, I lead the design process with a focus on user needs and business objectives. My approach combines strategic thinking with hands-on design skills, ensuring that every interaction is both intuitive and engaging.

In my leadership role, I mentor junior designers, foster collaboration, and drive the adoption of best practices in UX/UI design. I'm committed to staying at the forefront of design trends and technologies, continuously exploring new ways to elevate user experiences and drive product success.
